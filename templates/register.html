Terms of Service & Guidelines

1. Community Respect & Public Accountability

ActBayan is a dedicated digital platform designed for the residents of Barangay Picaleon. Users agree to maintain constructive, respectful communication when submitting community concerns or commenting on local reports.

2. Authenticity of Reported Concerns

All reports submitted through ActBayan must represent genuine barangay issues (e.g., infrastructure, safety, public utilities, waste management). Submitting false reports, misleading evidence, or defamatory content is strictly prohibited and may result in account suspension.

3. LGU Verification & Action

By filing a public concern, you authorize Barangay Picaleon officials and verified LGU personnel to inspect, verify, and track the status of the reported issue on the public community map.

Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)

1. Commitment to Resident Privacy

In full compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), ActBayan and the Local Government of Barangay Picaleon commit to safeguarding your personal records, contact details, and identity verification.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

Your personal data is collected exclusively for legitimate public governance: authenticating barangay residency, verifying reported community concerns, communicating direct resolution updates, and generating anonymized municipal analytics.

3. Data Security & Non-Disclosure

We implement robust administrative and technical encryption protocols to protect your records against unauthorized access. Your personal contact information will never be shared with unauthorized third parties or commercial advertisers.
