Background of the Study

Why General Mamerto Natividad Chose ACTBAYAN

A participatory digital platform designed to connect community observations with municipal action.

The Mission

“Every pothole reported. Every repair tracked. Every barangay heard.”

Residents of General Mamerto Natividad are often the very first to notice infrastructure needs — a pothole forming, a streetlight out, a drain clogging. ACTBAYAN gives that everyday awareness a direct, structured channel to the municipal government, replacing scattered paper notes with a single participatory digital system built for evidence-based, faster local governance.

Report Consolidation & Summarization

One of ACTBAYAN's key innovations is preventing system backlog when multiple residents report the same infrastructure defect.

1. Automated Duplicate Detection

When 10 residents report a pothole on the same street, the system groups them by GPS proximity instead of creating 10 cluttered tickets.

2. Concise AI/System Summaries

Generates a single unified report summary with consolidated photographic evidence, streamlining evaluation for LGU engineers.

3. Synchronized Community Updates

When the municipal repair team marks the consolidated ticket as resolved, all participating reporters receive instant verification.

CAPSTONE INNOVATION

ACTBAYAN's Six Core System Features

Designed to address municipal infrastructure monitoring challenges through technology and participatory governance.

1. Resident Reporting Module

Residents can report potholes, damaged roads, broken streetlights, clogged drains, and other infrastructure concerns.

2. LGU Management System

A dedicated portal for local officials and engineers to manage, prioritize, and respond to reports submitted by the community.

3. Centralized Data Analytics

Integrated analytical tools that track infrastructure decay patterns and help municipal leaders make data-driven decisions.

4. Transparency Dashboard

Public-facing visibility features providing community members with real-time updates into the progress of public works.

5. Geolocation Mapping

Integration with GPS services to capture exact coordinates of reported hazards, enabling precise tracking and dispatch.

6. Report Consolidation

An automated tool that identifies and groups duplicate reports from the same area into concise summaries to prevent backlog.

Participatory Process

How the Monitoring Process Works

Connecting community vigilance with municipal action in three structured steps.

1. Identify & GPS Tag

A resident notices a public works issue in General Mamerto Natividad, snaps a photo, and submits it. GPS coordinates are captured automatically.

2. Consolidate & Dispatch

The system groups any duplicate reports and routes a clean, summarized priority ticket directly to the responsible LGU engineering or maintenance team.

3. Resolve & Update

Repair crews complete the public works restoration. The Transparency Dashboard updates instantly, notifying all participating community members.
