Background of the Study

Why General Mamerto Natividad Needs ACTBAYAN

Transforming unstructured, manual inspection procedures into an integrated digital framework for efficient municipal governance.

Legacy Challenge

The Problem: Unstructured & Manual Monitoring

Historically, the monitoring of infrastructure in General Mamerto Natividad Municipality utilized unstructured procedures and processes carried out manually. Notes were taken arbitrarily during inspection rounds, making it difficult for local officials to follow up on changes and ensure information remained updated. While community residents are often the very first to identify infrastructure hazards—such as potholes, broken streetlights, or clogged drains—the lack of a structured reporting channel caused repair workflows to suffer from severe delays and backlogs.

Arbitrary Notes Severe Delays Unstructured Channel
ACTBAYAN Innovation

Our Solution: An Integrated Participatory Framework

ACTBAYAN bridges this gap by serving as a dedicated participatory public works monitoring system. By connecting community residents directly with the centralized municipal administration, paper-based practices are replaced with digital communication channels. The platform incorporates real-time reporting, automated duplicate consolidation, GPS coordinate tagging, and data-driven analytical tools to ensure evidence-based decision-making and rapid LGU response times across all 20 barangays.

Report Consolidation & Summarization

One of ACTBAYAN's key innovations is preventing system backlog when multiple residents report the same infrastructure defect.

1. Automated Duplicate Detection

When 10 residents report a pothole on the same street, the system groups them by GPS proximity instead of creating 10 cluttered tickets.

2. Concise AI/System Summaries

Generates a single unified report summary with consolidated photographic evidence, streamlining evaluation for LGU engineers.

3. Synchronized Community Updates

When the municipal repair team marks the consolidated ticket as resolved, all participating reporters receive instant verification.

Capstone Innovation

ACTBAYAN's Six Core System Features

Designed specifically to solve municipal infrastructure monitoring challenges through structured technology and participatory governance.

1. Resident Reporting Module

An online tool allowing community residents to report specific infrastructure issues, such as potholes, broken streetlights, damaged roads, and clogged drains.

2. LGU Management System

A dedicated administrative portal for local officials and municipal engineers to manage, process, prioritize, and respond to reports submitted by the community.

3. Centralized Data Analytics

Integrated analytical tools that track infrastructure decay patterns and help municipal leaders make data-driven, evidence-based budgetary choices.

4. Transparency Dashboard

Public-facing visibility features providing community members with real-time updates into the progress, status, and completion of public works projects.

5. Geolocation Mapping

Integration with GPS services to automatically capture exact coordinates of reported hazards, enabling precise tracking and dispatch without relying on manual descriptions.

6. Report Consolidation

An automated tool that identifies and groups duplicate reports from the same area into concise summaries, preventing clutter and reducing LGU backlog.

Participatory Process

How the Monitoring Process Works

Connecting community vigilance with municipal action in three structured steps.

1. Identify & GPS Tag

A resident notices a public works issue in General Mamerto Natividad, snaps a photo, and submits it. GPS coordinates are captured automatically.

2. Consolidate & Dispatch

The system groups any duplicate reports and routes a clean, summarized priority ticket directly to the responsible LGU engineering or maintenance team.

3. Resolve & Update

Repair crews complete the public works restoration. The Transparency Dashboard updates instantly, notifying all participating community members.
