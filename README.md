# ACTBAYAN-CAPSTONE
Design and Development of ACTBAYAN: A Participatory Public Works Monitoring System for  General Mamerto Natividad, Nueva Ecija

A well-organized public sector plays a crucial role in shaping the quality of life in Nueva Ecija, where the effectiveness of routine activities depends largely on the reliability of supporting infrastructure (Siar, 2007). This relationship underscores how community stability is closely tied to the condition and dependability of physical systems. Among these, road networks stand out as critical drivers of development.
